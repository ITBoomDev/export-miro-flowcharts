# Privacy Policy — Export Miro Flowcharts

_Last updated: 2026-11-21_

**Export Miro Flowcharts collects no data and sends nothing anywhere.**

## What the extension does with your data

- It reads the flowchart items on the Miro board tab you have open (shapes, text, connectors, selection),
  inside your browser, only when you open the side panel or press the copy hotkey.
- It converts them to text (LLM text, Mermaid or JSON) locally and puts the result in the side panel,
  on your clipboard, or in a file you download.
- It stores your preferences (format, options, language, prompt templates) in `chrome.storage.local`
  on your device. This data never leaves your browser and is not synced.

## What it does not do

- No network requests of any kind. No servers, no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting.
- No remote code: everything that runs is inside the extension package.
- No access to any site other than `https://miro.com/app/board/*`.
- No selling, sharing or transferring of user data, because none is collected.

## Your control

Uninstalling the extension removes all stored preferences. You can also clear them at any time from
`chrome://extensions` → the extension → "Clear data".

Questions or concerns: open an issue in the project's GitHub repository.
